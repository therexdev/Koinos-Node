External producer wallet

Producer Signer

Sign with Kondor on your secure computer. Your producer private key stays in the wallet.

On the node computer, open Node → Producer wallet custody → External signing, prepare a transaction and download the unsigned JSON. Bring that file here. You do not need KoinosKit installed on this computer.

1. Load and review

Network: Koinos Mainnet. KoinosKit uses Mainnet by default. For Harbinger, use the offline helper.

Or paste unsigned JSON

Choose the unsigned file exported by KoinosKit.

Decoded operations and contract addresses

      
    

  

  

    
2. Sign in Kondor

    
Use the browser where Kondor is installed. Approve access to the producer account, then review Kondor's transaction prompt. Keep Use free mana off. Optimize mana can stay on if its final Max mana is within the prepared maximum.

    
    
This page requests a signature, then checks the returned transaction. Kondor may contact its RPC to simulate the transaction. KoinosKit on your node computer performs the final broadcast after your confirmation.

  

  

    
3. Return to KoinosKit

    
After signing, download the signed JSON and take it back to the node computer.

    
    
Signed JSON — copy if needed

    
Choose Import signed JSON in KoinosKit, review and confirm, then broadcast. For registration, wait for confirmation and click Verify registration before starting production.

  

  
Drafts expire after 15 minutes, or 24 hours when prepared with Dual-boot / offline signing. After returning to Windows, choose Resume saved draft in KoinosKit before importing the signed JSON. Protect signed files: the blockchain does not enforce this local expiry. Files are processed in this page without uploading them to KoinosKit's website. Never enter a WIF or private key here.
